Conclusions Prediction error; lower is better Normalizing to the same radius changes each matrix’s effective weights: d W = ρ target W/ρ(W ). A shared CE multiplier reduces, but does not remove, the control advantage. Biological wiring is not a general advantage here. 1. CE wins limited conditions against binary ER. Stronger weight-matched controls remove its MC advantage under own-radius scaling. 2. CE weight placement and topology limit mean performance. Its magnitude distribution remains useful when transferred to ER. 3. A matched gamma improves ER memory and prediction. Distributional shape matters beyond the first two moments.
